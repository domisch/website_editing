We constantly seek possible collaborations regarding interesting projects, novel methods, data, and publications in spatial freshwater biodiversity science and beyond. Please do not hesitate to [contact us](mailto:sami.domisch@igb-berlin.de?subject=[glowabio]%20contact) to discuss further details.
<br/>

__Join the group?__

Are you interested in joining the group? We are looking for motivated candidates across the Master, PhD and Postdoc levels.
If you are interested please [drop us a line](mailto:sami.domisch@igb-berlin.de?subject=[glowabio]%20contact) indicating your research interests & plans and we can discuss how to move things further and seek for possible funding opportunities.

<br/>

We use, beside R, largely open-source (command-line) GIS tools within the Linux environment that facilitate handling and analyzing large datasets (see also some of the tools [here](/data_code/open-source-gis)). We consider a good understanding, or alternatively, a great motivation to learn such tools & techniques as a prerequisite to join the group.
