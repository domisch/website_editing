Freshwater habitats and biodiversity require efficient protection measures calling for detailed information regarding the spatial distribution of freshwater environmental characteristics. We developed a data-driven approach to identify environmental characteristics and similarities between freshwater habitats at high-resolution and across large spatial scales. We aggregated land cover, geomorphological and climatic variables across the sub-catchments of the Hydrography90m dataset in six drainage basins across continents and climatic zones. We then employed a k-means cluster analysis and tested the effect of (i) spatial scale, (ii) the choice of environmental variables, and (iii) the combination of scale and variables, on the resulting habitat regionalization.
