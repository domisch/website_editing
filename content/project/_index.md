We are currently focusing on several topics:

- [Creating novel high-resolution freshwater ecoregions](/project/glowabio_habitats)
- [Analyzing spatial patterns in global freshwater biodiversity](/project/glowabio_biodiversity)
- [Spatial conservation planning given habitat and biodiversity features](/project//glowabio_conservation)

.. and are part of the following collaborations:
- [Global taxonomic, functional and phylogenetic diversity of stream macroinvertebrate communities](https://www.largescaleecologylab.net/) - lead by Jani Heino
- [Niche evolution of Tibetan-Himalayan Rhyacophilidae (Trichoptera)](http://gepris.dfg.de/gepris/projekt/224580917?language=en) - lead by Steffen Pauls
- Protecting the future of freshwater biodiversity and ecosystem services across Cuba - lead by Yusdiel Torres-Cambas

.. and larger research projects and networks:
- [Environmental risk of chemicals in the future](https://ecorisk2050.eu/)
- [Alliance for Freshwater Life](https://allianceforfreshwaterlife.org/)
- [Freshwater Biodiversity Observation Network](https://geobon.org/bons/thematic-bon/freshwater-bon/)
- [IPCC WGII Sixth Assessment Report](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/sixth-assessment-report-working-group-ii/)
