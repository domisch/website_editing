Alejandra Correa-Bedoya is a Doctoral student at the [Leibniz Institute of Freshwater Ecology and Inland Fisheries (IGB)](https://www.igb-berlin.de/en/).She focuses on assessing aquatic insects distribution patterns across the Magdalena-Cauca River Basin in Colombia, thereby identifying key biodiversity regions and evaluating habitat fragmentation from existing and planned hydropower dams.
