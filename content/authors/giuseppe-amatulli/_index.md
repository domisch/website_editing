Dr. Giuseppe Amatulli is a research scientist at Yale University and focuses on large-scale & open-source geocomputation. He has been at the forefront of the the Geomorpho90m and Hydrography90m datasets and is actively enganged in teaching the latest advances in geocomputation and machine learning.
